# Access level: standard
## Options per question
3-4 clickable options plus "tell me more". Recommended option first.
## Sitting length
One long sitting is fine. Offer a break every 2 interview areas ("Shall we keep going or pause here?").
## Answer style
Short answers, the answer first, one idea per line. Mention Win+H (speak instead of typing) once in stage 1 and once in stage 3.
