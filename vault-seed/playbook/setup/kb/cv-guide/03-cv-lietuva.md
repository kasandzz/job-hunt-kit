# Your CV in Lithuania (Gyvenimo aprašymas)

Instructions in English; say everything to the person in Lithuanian, with the voice in `playbook/profiles/language-lt-LT.md`.

## In short
- One or two pages. Newest job first.
- Write it in Lithuanian. Make an English copy only if the advert is in English.
- A short profile at the top: 2-3 lines on who you are and what you want.
- Languages, each with a level (Lithuanian, Russian, English, Polish and so on): employers often ask.
- What you achieved, with numbers.
- A photo only if you want one.
- Never the personal code (asmens kodas), birth date, age or marital status.

## More detail
- **Shape:** contact details (name, town, phone or email), a short profile, work history newest first, education, languages, skills (for example computer programs, driving licence category). (SELF-REPORTED: Lithuanian job sites' CV advice)
- **Photo:** many CVs in Lithuania have one, but it is not required. (SELF-REPORTED: Lithuanian job sites' CV advice)
- **Older jobs:** the last 15 years in detail; earlier jobs can go in one short line. (INFERRED: the same advice as in Mexico and the UK)
- **Europass:** some people use the EU's Europass CV form. It is fine but long; a simple CV is usually better. (SELF-REPORTED: Europass)
- **Help in person:** Užimtumo tarnyba, the state employment service, helps people of any age find work and is free. (SELF-REPORTED: uzt.lt)
