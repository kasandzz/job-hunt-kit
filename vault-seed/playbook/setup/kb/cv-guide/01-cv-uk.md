# Your CV in the UK

## In short
- Two pages at most. Newest job first.
- A short profile at the top: 2-3 lines on who you are and what you want.
- What you achieved, with numbers.
- No photo. No date of birth. No age.

## More detail
- **Shape:** contact details (name, town, phone or email), a short profile, work history newest first, education, skills. (SELF-REPORTED: UK CV guides)
- **No photo, no date of birth:** UK employers do not expect them, and leaving them out keeps the focus on your work. (SELF-REPORTED: UK CV guides)
- **Age:** age is one of the things the law protects against unfair treatment at work. (SELF-REPORTED: UK government guidance, page not opened in this review)
- **Older jobs:** the last 15 years in detail; earlier jobs can go in one short line. (INFERRED: the same advice as in Mexico)
