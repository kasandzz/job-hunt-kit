# Start here

## What this is
A helper on your computer that helps you find work. It looks for jobs every day, tells you which ones fit, gets your CV ready and helps you apply. Nothing goes without your OK, twice.

The helper is called **Claude**. Claude is a program you chat with by typing or talking, like messaging a very patient friend. It also makes you a notebook on your computer, in a program called **Obsidian**. You don't need to know how any of it works.

## Before you start
- A paid Claude plan in your name.
- Your old CV, if you have one: on paper, as a photo or as a file. If you don't, that's fine.
- About 90 minutes. You can stop any time and carry on later.

## The easy way
You were sent some text to paste. Open the Claude app, click **Code**, pick your **Documents** folder, paste the text and press Enter. Claude does the rest and tells you each step.

## If you were sent a zip file
1. Right-click the file whose name ends in **.zip**.
2. Choose **Extract All**, then **Extract**.
3. In the new folder, double-click **Install**. If a blue box says **Windows protected your PC**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
4. Wait for the box that says **All done**.

## Then
1. In the Claude app, click **Code**.
2. Pick the **My-Life** folder in Documents.
3. Type **Start** and press Enter.
- To talk instead of typing, press the Windows key and H together.
- If a box asks for permission and Claude has just told you why, click **Allow**.
- Need a break? Type **pause**. Type **carry on** to pick up again.

## Your privacy
Your notes stay on your computer, and in a private copy online that only you can see. Nothing is sent without you: Claude shows you everything first and asks for your OK twice. You choose who presses the last button; on LinkedIn it is always you.
